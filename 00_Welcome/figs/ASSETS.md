# Welcome figures

Source: `../History_of_Fire_Modeling_4.pptx` (64 slides). Asset names retain the
PowerPoint media identifiers for tracing back to the original deck.

Photographs, screenshots, and video posters retain their original resolution.
TIFF images were converted to PNG. Windows EMF and WMF charts, equations, and
line drawings were converted to SVG; their lines and text remain vector.
Original photographs inside mixed-content vector figures remain raster.
The photo collages `image67`, `image69`, and `image70` are PNG, not raster
images wrapped in SVG. `image128.jpg` has its EXIF orientation normalized;
the slide applies the source PowerPoint rotation.

The dimensionless scaling, puffing-frequency, and MQH equations use editable
LaTeX in the section files. Existing figure credits and slide notes are retained.
Legacy Windows fonts use browser-available substitutes. The original deck is
retained locally for comparison.

Video posters live here. The clips live in `../videos/` and are prepared for the
repository's `2026` GitHub Release; see `../videos/manifest.csv`.
